How CheckMate Works



CheckMate uses advanced AI to scan, grade, and generate results for multiple-choice tests — saving you hours of manual checking.

Step 1: Create or Upload the Answer Key



Simply create or upload your test’s official answer key to CheckMate. The system uses this to compare student answers with the correct choices.

Step 2: Scan Test Papers



Snap a photo or upload scanned copies of completed multiple-choice sheets. Our AI automatically detects and reads answers — no templates needed!

Step 3: Instant AI Grading



CheckMate instantly scores each paper, highlights incorrect answers, and calculates total scores. It’s fast, accurate, and completely automated.

Step 4: Review and Export



Review results in a clean, easy-to-navigate dashboard. Download results for recordkeeping or grade encoding.
